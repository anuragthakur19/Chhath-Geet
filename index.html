About Chhath

Chhath Puja is a four-day festival of the sun god Surya and Chhathi Maiya, celebrated widely in Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh, Nepal's Terai and by diaspora families around the world. Devotees fast, bathe in rivers or ponds, and offer arghya to the setting and rising sun.

Its songs are a large part of its character. Many are folk geet passed down by women through generations, and they are sung during cooking, walking to the ghat, and standing in the water.
